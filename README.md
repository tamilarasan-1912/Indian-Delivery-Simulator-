# Indian Delivery Simulator

A 3D delivery-partner simulation game inspired by the real delivery workflow in India.

## Prototype V0.1

Core loop:

1. Wait for an order
2. Receive and accept the order
3. Travel to the store
4. Pick up and verify the package
5. Travel to the customer
6. Contact the customer
7. Verify OTP
8. Collect COD or confirm online payment
9. Scan and hand over the package
10. Complete the delivery and receive earnings/rating

## Planned systems

- First-person delivery rider gameplay
- Motorcycle riding and traffic
- Store pickup workflow
- Customer interactions and calls
- OTP verification
- Cash-on-delivery and change calculation
- Order/earnings system
- Indian-style city environment
- Dynamic customer and traffic AI
- Career progression and vehicle upgrades

## Technology

- Unity 6 LTS
- C#
- Git/GitHub
- Blender for bespoke 3D assets

## Development principle

Build a small playable vertical slice first, then expand the city, AI, economy, content, and career systems incrementally.
